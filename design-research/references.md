## The synthesis
Personaudit's subject is evidence handed over in a file. The best references from outside the industry (Tufte, Field Notes, Pudding) share three habits: **serial numbers on everything**, **marginal annotation**, and **plain labeled structure**. The competitors share the opposite: purple/blue gradients, a logo wall, stock photos, "Book a demo". The direction below borrows the three habits.
